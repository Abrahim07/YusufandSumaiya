# Hero photos

Save the two example images you shared into this folder with these exact names:

- `groom.png` — the Muslim man (shown on the left of the hero)
- `bride.png` — the Muslim woman (shown on the right of the hero)

Recommended: tall portrait images (roughly 800×1200 or larger). PNG with a
transparent or plain background works best, but any JPG/PNG will display.

Until these files are added, the hero shows the Medina skyline and warm dawn
sky on its own — the empty photo frames hide automatically.
