Prophetic Guidance

The Virtue & Importance of Attending the Walimah

1. Accepting the walimah invitation

The Prophet ﷺ said:

“When one of you is invited to a walimah, let him attend it.”

— Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim

The wording in Sahih Muslim is:

“If any one of you is invited to a wedding feast, he should accept it.”
Sahih Muslim 1429a
